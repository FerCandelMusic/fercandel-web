## About me

I've spent many years bringing music to unforgettable moments. Weddings, corporate events, terraces, restaurants — the saxophone has something that transforms any space.

I play tenor saxophone with a repertoire ranging from Soul and Jazz to Blues, Rock, Pop, ballads and House, always tailored to what your event needs. I'm based in Madrid and Cádiz, and I travel to Seville, Málaga and Huelva.

## FAQ

### What kind of events can I book you for?

Weddings (ceremony and cocktail hour), corporate events, hotels, restaurants and terraces.

### What music do you play?

Soul, Jazz, Blues, Rock, Pop, ballads and House, on tenor saxophone with high-quality backing tracks. I tailor the repertoire to each event.

### Can I request specific songs?

Yes. I need a couple of weeks to prepare them properly.

### Do you bring your own sound system?

Yes, my own high-quality, powerful sound system, proven at many performances.

### How long is a performance?

Usually between 90 and 120 minutes.

### Where do you perform?

I'm based in Madrid and Cádiz, and I travel to Seville, Málaga and Huelva. A surcharge may apply for long-distance travel.

### Do you play outdoors?

Yes. I just need a power outlet and, if possible, some shade.

### How far in advance should I book?

The sooner the better, but I can accommodate last-minute requests.

### How much does it cost?

From €500, depending on the event. [Get in touch](https://fercandel.com/en/#contacto) and I'll prepare a no-obligation quote.

### Do you also play with a band?

Yes. Among other projects, with “Y sin embargo…”, a tribute show to Joaquín Sabina; “Distractions”; “Shangri-la Social Band” and “Rosci Neuence”, a Madrid-based Spanish pop band with original songs.
